## What To Emphasize In Interviews

This is the repository that shows mathematical maturity. It should not be pitched as finance. It should be pitched as the foundation underneath quantitative research.

A clean explanation:

> I wanted my profile not to be only backtests and portfolio projects. So I built a separate mathematical repo around stochastic processes: random walks, martingales, stopping times, quadratic variation, and Brownian-motion convergence. The idea was to show the probability-theory layer underneath systematic research before moving into alpha diagnostics and portfolio construction.

## What Not To Say

Avoid describing it as:

- a trading strategy;
- an investment model;
- a portfolio project;
- a market prediction tool.

The strength of the repository is precisely that it is not trying to be finance. It is the pure mathematical layer.
